Super Duper Potions!

The best ingredient list beings on the mountain.

Forest Foraging: is your best bet for potion ingredients~ rare herbs, glowing mushrooms, and critter-shed materials tend to spawn near old trees and damp moss beds. Most high tier reagent hide in shaded pockets where players don't usually wander. If you see fireflies, you are close.

Mountain Cliffs: drop the strongest elemental ingredients- crystal shards, storm touched flowers, and anything infused with wind or lightening. Check ledges after weather events because fresh nodes spawn there. The rare the terrain, the better the yield.

Swamp Zones: are perfect for transformation potions thanks to toxic reeds, sledge bulbs, and amphibian essence. Ingredients here respawn fast but require careful timing since some only appear at night. If the water looks suspiciously green, that's the jackpot.
